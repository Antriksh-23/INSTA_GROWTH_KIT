## Prompt 1 — Clean Background Enhancement (use with `photo-01.jpg` or `photo-05.jpg`)

```
Enhance this WHOOP 5.0 fitness tracker product photo for an Instagram ad.

Requirements:
- Improve the lighting to look professional and well-lit
- Replace the background with a clean, dark gradient background (black to dark grey — matching WHOOP's premium brand aesthetic)
- Keep the WHOOP device, band, logo, text, and colours EXACTLY as they are — do not alter, blur, or replace any part of the product itself
- Maintain the original product shape and proportions
- Make the WHOOP 5.0 device the clear focal point
- Output in high resolution suitable for Instagram (1080px width minimum)
```

## Prompt 2 — Lifestyle Background (use with `photo-04.jpg` for wrist-on context)

```
Place this WHOOP 5.0 fitness tracker in a lifestyle setting for an Instagram ad.

Requirements:
- Add a fitness/active lifestyle background (gym, running, yoga, or outdoor workout setting)
- Use dynamic, energetic lighting that conveys an active lifestyle
- The WHOOP device, band, logo, all text, and colours must remain EXACTLY unchanged
- Do not modify, obscure, or replace any part of the actual product
- Keep the WHOOP 5.0 as the main focus — background should complement, not distract
- High resolution output (1080px+)
```

## Prompt 3 — Premium Product Shot (use with `photo-01.jpg` or `photo-04.jpg`)

```
Transform this WHOOP 5.0 fitness tracker photo into a premium e-commerce style shot.

Requirements:
- Studio-quality lighting with subtle shadows
- Clean, minimal dark background (solid black or very subtle dark gradient — matching WHOOP's brand identity)
- Product must look exactly as it is — same WHOOP logo, same band colour, same device shape
- Do NOT change, edit, or enhance the product label or packaging in any way
- Add a slight reflection/shadow beneath the product for a premium feel
- Output at Instagram-ready resolution (1080px+)
```

---

## ⚠️ Important Reminders

- **Always verify** the output: zoom in and check that the WHOOP logo, device screen, band colour, and overall product appearance haven't been altered
- If Gemini changes any part of the WHOOP device or band, regenerate or manually correct
- Save both the original photo AND the enhanced version for before/after comparison
- These enhanced images are the BASE — you'll add text overlays (price, offer, CTA) in **Canva** next
