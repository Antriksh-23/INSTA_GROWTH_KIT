# Canva Ad Layout Guide — WHOOP 5.0 | Insta Growth Kit

> Step-by-step guide for building 3 WHOOP 5.0 ad images in Canva after AI-enhancing your photos.

---

## Setup

1. Open [Canva](https://www.canva.com/) (free account works)
2. Create two custom-size designs:
   - **Instagram Reel Cover:** 1080 × 1920 px
   - **Instagram Post:** 1080 × 1080 px

---

## Layout Template for Each Ad

### Elements to Include

| Element | Position | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Product image** | Centre / hero area | Upload your Gemini-enhanced WHOOP 5.0 photo |
| **Price sticker** | Top-right or bottom-left corner | Bold, high-contrast (e.g., dark badge with white text: "From ₹20,890") |
| **Offer text** | Below or beside price | "No-Cost EMI Available!" — use brand-approved wording only |
| **Brand/shop name** | Top or bottom bar | "WHOOP" — consistent across all 3 ads |
| **CTA text** | Bottom area | "Comment PRICE below 👇" — must be present on at least 1 image |

### Design Tips

- **Font size:** Price and CTA text should be readable on a phone screen (minimum 40pt in Canva)
- **Colours:** Use 2–3 colours max; match your brand palette
- **Whitespace:** Don't overcrowd — let the product breathe
- **Consistency:** All 3 ads should look like they belong to the same series (same fonts, same colour scheme)

---

## Ad-by-Ad Instructions

### Ad 1 — Hero Shot
1. Upload `photo-01.jpg` (clean product shot) — enhance with Gemini first for a dark gradient bg
2. Add a dark/black top bar with "WHOOP" branding (matches their premium aesthetic)
3. Add price sticker — "Starting ₹20,890" (corner badge style)
4. Add a thin bottom strip with "Visit whoop.in" or "DM us for details"
5. Export as `ad-01-9x16.png` and `ad-01-1x1.png`

### Ad 2 — Offer Highlight
1. Upload `photo-05.jpg` (device + app showing metrics) — great for showcasing features + price
2. Use a bold, large text block for the offer: "No-Cost EMI Available!"
3. Show both tiers: "WHOOP One ₹20,890 | WHOOP Peak ₹27,539"
4. Add "Comment PRICE below 👇" as CTA text at the bottom
5. Consider adding urgency: "Save up to ₹1,451 on MRP!"
6. Export as `ad-02-9x16.png` and `ad-02-1x1.png`

### Ad 3 — Lifestyle / Benefits
1. Upload `photo-02.jpg` (Virat Kohli) or `photo-03.jpg` (Ronaldo) for aspirational lifestyle appeal
2. Add 2–3 benefit bullets (e.g., "✅ Screen-Free 24/7 Tracking", "✅ 14+ Day Battery", "✅ AI WHOOP Coach")
3. Add subtle price: "From ₹20,890"
4. Add WHOOP logo and whoop.in URL
5. ⚠️ Verify celebrity image usage rights before publishing in paid ads
6. Export as `ad-03-9x16.png` and `ad-03-1x1.png`

---

## Export Settings

- **File type:** PNG (recommended for quality) or JPG
- **Quality:** Maximum
- **Naming:** Follow the convention in `ad-image-specs.md`
- **Save to:** `creative/ad-images/` folder

---

## Before/After Image

1. Create a new design (any size — 1080 × 1080 recommended)
2. Place the **original `photo-01.jpg`** (product shot) on the left half
3. Place the **final Ad 1 image** on the right half
4. Add labels: "Before" and "After"
5. Save as `creative/ad-images/before-after.png`
