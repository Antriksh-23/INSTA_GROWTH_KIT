## 🎙️ AI Voice Generation Pro-Tips (To Kill the Robot Accent)

1. **Write numbers phonetically:**  
   If the AI voice says *"Rupee symbol two zero eight nine zero"*, change text to:  
   `bees hazaar aath sau nabbe` OR `20,890 rupees`.
2. **Remove stiff punctuation:**  
   Use dashes (`—`) and commas where you want the voice to pause and breathe naturally, like a human speaking.
3. **Voice Persona Prompt (if using Google AI Studio / Gemini TTS):**  
   > *"Speak in a relaxed, energetic Indian Gen-Z/creator voice. Casual, confident, speaking to a friend, not reading a script."*
4. **Target Audio File:** Save finalized output to [`creative/voiceover.mp3`](./voiceover.mp3).
